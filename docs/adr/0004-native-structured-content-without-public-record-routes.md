# Native structured records without public record routes

Exactly two plugin-owned custom post types, Service and Project, provide admin-editable structured records reused across the existing Home, Services, Gallery, and Contact pages. Services supply repeated offering content and appointment choices; Projects supply repeated work examples and photography. Neither type has public single routes or archives, and no additional custom post type is approved.

Fixed page-specific sections use registered page metadata and native meta boxes; small fixed collections such as homepage testimonials use structured/repeatable metadata. The plugin owns content registration and validation, while the theme owns presentation. Native site identity and WordPress menus remain authoritative, and a project settings screen holds genuinely shared business data. There is no ACF dependency, visual page builder, or requirement to make every UI string configurable: form labels, validation messages, button text, and other interface strings remain code-owned/translatable unless a demonstrated editorial need justifies otherwise.

Services and Projects have stable machine identifiers independent of editable display titles and WordPress post slugs. They support fixture/import mapping and cross-page references; Service identifiers also support CTA preselection and server-side form validation. Anchors derive from stable identifiers so editorial renaming cannot break links. Native page/menu references generate routes; Service links target Services sections and Project links target Gallery sections. This model deliberately avoids separate record pages in order to preserve frozen public scope. Legal destinations must be explicitly configured before production acceptance; the person icon remains decorative and appropriately excluded from assistive interaction.

The detailed Services page supplies initial canonical service facts for exactly Exterior Detail, Interior Detail, Full Detail, Paint Correction, and Ceramic Coating. Home's Interior & Exterior Detail maps to Full Detail, and Paint Correction uses 2–3 days everywhere. Prices, durations, and protection periods come from canonical records only where those facts exist in the frozen baseline; missing commercial or technical claims must not be invented. Only published and valid Services appear in public listings and appointment choices.

One Project record represents each distinct work example. Page-specific ordered references place Projects on Home, Gallery, or both; contextual teasers and alternate imagery preserve the frozen presentation while shared vehicle/work facts remain authoritative. The shared Porsche is one record, while Home's Aston Martin DB12 and Range Rover SV remain distinct from Gallery's Aston Martin DBS and other projects. Gallery group/filter assignments remain limited to the exported baseline and are not inferred from descriptive prose.

Administrators and Editors may manage page editorial content, Services, Projects, and their editorial metadata. Only Administrators manage shared business settings, legal destinations, the appointment notification recipient, and other operational configuration; mail transport credentials remain environment configuration. Enable and test native revisions for supported editorial records and revision-enabled metadata, including restoration. Shared settings need documented backup/recovery, not a new audit-log system.
