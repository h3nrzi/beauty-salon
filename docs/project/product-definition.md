# Product definition

## Owner and outcome

**Selected product direction:** Beauty Booking Platform for a single beauty salon.

The product is more than a marketing website. Its core value is a real appointment-booking experience for salon customers.

Working customer journey:

```text
Discover salon/services
→ choose a service
→ choose a specialist
→ choose a date and available time
→ confirm the appointment
→ later view/manage upcoming appointments
```

Owner and final acceptance owner: Pending confirmation.

Primary customer, exact salon model, and the business outcome are still being defined.

## Scope

Confirmed direction:

- One salon business, not a marketplace.
- A public salon experience plus a real booking flow.
- Customers should be able to book an appointment rather than only submit a lead.
- The booking experience is expected to involve service, specialist, date/time and confirmation.
- Customer appointment management is part of the product direction; the exact actions still need to be agreed.

Still to define:

- Exact salon type and target audience.
- Whether users must sign in before booking or can begin/book as guests.
- Which appointment-management actions are in scope.
- Which public pages/journeys are required.
- Whether staff/admin operational workflows are part of this project.
- Explicit exclusions for the first release.

Do not choose WordPress architecture, content types, storage, authentication implementation, form infrastructure or other engineering details during this phase.

## Constraints

Pending product decisions:

- Language and locale.
- Brand direction and content inputs.
- Responsive/mobile expectations.
- Accessibility target.
- Privacy expectations around customer/appointment information.
- Asset ownership/rights.
- Business rules that affect booking, such as advance horizon, cancellation or rescheduling policy.

Unknowns remain explicit until agreed.

## Acceptance planning

Local, human and production-release acceptance will be separated before engineering specification.

At this stage, product acceptance means the agreed customer journeys, pages, actions, constraints and exclusions are explicit enough to create the Stitch brief without inventing product behavior.
