# Product definition

## Owner and outcome

Who owns scope and acceptance? Who uses this product, and what outcome do they need?

## Scope

List journeys, pages, primary actions, content and editing needs. State exclusions
explicitly. Define observable success criteria without choosing an architecture.

## Constraints

Record brand/content inputs, asset rights, accessibility needs, languages,
responsive intent, privacy/reliability needs and known runtime or delivery constraints.
Mark unknowns explicitly rather than filling them with assumptions.

## Acceptance planning

Identify local checks, human acceptance owners and likely release prerequisites.
Record capability gaps now; settle detailed gates before engineering specification.

## Scope approval

Record owner, date, approved scope and unresolved questions. Generate the Stitch
brief only after product scope is agreed. Record later changes explicitly.
