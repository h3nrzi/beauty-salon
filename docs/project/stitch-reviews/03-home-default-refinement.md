The refined Home is substantially stronger than the placeholder baseline.

## Remaining semantic/content blockers

### 1. “First / fastest available” specialist wording is wrong

The Home says:

`انتخاب متخصص یا اولین وقت`

and implies booking the fastest available slot.

The frozen product supports:

- a specific specialist; or
- **any available specialist**.

It does not define “first available” / “fastest available” as the product concept.

Use:

`انتخاب متخصص یا هر متخصص در دسترس`

### 2. Simultaneous-service wording contradicts the booking model

The Home says:

`زمان‌بندی پیوسته خدمات همزمان`

The product rule is:

- multiple services are performed **consecutively**;
- one specialist performs the complete service set.

Use wording such as:

`زمان‌بندی پیوسته خدمات پشت‌سرهم`

Do not imply parallel/simultaneous services.

### 3. Reminder capability was invented

The Home says appointment management includes:

`امکان یادآوری`

No reminder/notification feature is frozen in v1.

Use:

- view upcoming appointments;
- manage allowed changes;
- appointment history.

Do not promise reminders or notifications.

### 4. Unsupported salon/business claims remain

Examples include:

- “single exclusive branch in north Tehran” positioning;
- ventilation / quality claims;
- “selected/exclusive environment” language;
- precise neighborhood/business positioning presented as truth.

This is page refinement now, so replace those with neutral placeholder copy that does not claim unsupported business facts.

### 5. Material / service-treatment claims should remain neutral

Examples include wording around:

- moisturizing massage;
- “specialized” beauty-treatment language;
- material-quality / service-result claims.

Use neutral beauty-service placeholder descriptions.

### 6. Specialist schedule facts are too specific for the refined baseline

Cards include exact attendance days.

These may remain as clearly fictional fixture data, but they should not read like approved salon facts.

Prefer simpler placeholder expertise + eligible service presentation on Home.

### 7. Benefit copy overstates price certainty

The Home says there will be no unexpected/extra charges at the salon.

The frozen rule is narrower:

- service prices are shown;
- booking total is retained at confirmation;
- payment occurs at the salon.

Use the actual product benefit rather than a broader billing guarantee.

## Freeze gate

Keep the current Home layout and visual system.

One semantic/content cleanup pass is needed.

If the corrected Home preserves the current composition while removing the product drift above, **Home default state can be frozen**.
