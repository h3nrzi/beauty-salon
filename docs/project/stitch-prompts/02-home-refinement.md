# Stitch Prompt 02 — Home refinement

Refine the existing **آرا / Soft Editorial Home page**. Do not redesign from scratch and do not change product scope.

Preserve what already works:
- Persian/RTL composition;
- warm ivory/taupe/terracotta visual language;
- calm feminine premium tone;
- editorial whitespace;
- natural photography;
- clear price/duration presentation;
- specialist preview;
- gallery preview;
- pay-at-salon messaging.

Make the following corrections.

## 1. Make Home curated

The Home page is a discovery page, not the full Services page.

- Remove service category filters from Home.
- Show only 3 representative selected services.
- Keep name, short description, duration and price concise.
- Provide a clear «مشاهده همه خدمات» destination.
- Keep a direct booking CTA for each selected service if visually appropriate.

Do not introduce new product categories or packages.

## 2. Remove unsupported claims

Rewrite placeholder copy so it does not claim:
- 100% organic products;
- vegan sourcing;
- international certificates;
- clinical/medical status;
- guaranteed hygiene standards;
- awards;
- absolute safety claims;
- unsupported years of experience;
- unapproved packages.

Use grounded placeholder copy centered on care, clarity, professional service, transparent time/price and calm experience.

## 3. Represent booking truthfully

Do not say the booking is “only 3 steps” if that hides required product stages.

Use a concise visual summary that remains compatible with:

```text
انتخاب خدمات
→ انتخاب متخصص یا هر متخصص در دسترس
→ انتخاب تاریخ و ساعت
→ تأیید شماره موبایل
→ مرور و تأیید نهایی
```

Do not promise SMS delivery or name an SMS/provider behavior.

Clearly retain:
- pay at salon;
- no online payment.

## 4. Correct specialist wording

Use **«هر متخصص در دسترس»** or an equivalent phrase.

Do not use «اولین متخصص در دسترس» because no “first available” assignment rule is approved.

## 5. Fix mobile navigation

Mobile is a first-class target.

Create a clear mobile header/navigation pattern with:
- brand «آرا»;
- mobile menu trigger;
- access to خدمات، متخصص‌ها، گالری، درباره ما، تماس;
- discoverable «وقت‌های من»;
- prominent «رزرو وقت».

Do not hide My Appointments on mobile.

## 6. Fix Home navigation state

Do not mark «خدمات» as the active/current page on Home.

Either:
- include «خانه» and mark it current; or
- use the brand as Home and leave the other page links inactive.

## 7. Brand mark

Remove the portrait/profile-style image from the header brand area.

Use:
- the «آرا» wordmark;
- or a simple non-personal monogram/abstract mark.

Do not make the brand look like a user account/avatar.

## 8. Footer cleanup

Keep only product-supported destinations and clearly mock business details.

Do not present uncreated legal pages, client-rights charters, health certifications or unsupported policy destinations as real existing content.

If legal/privacy links are needed for layout, mark them clearly as placeholder content.

## 9. Persian-first typography

Keep Vazirmatn or another clearly Persian/Arabic-capable UI face for functional text.

For editorial headings, use a Persian-capable display treatment. Avoid relying on a Latin-only serif that produces uncontrolled Persian fallback.

Do not lock a production font implementation; this remains visual exploration.

## 10. Simplify accent tokens

Use one clear terracotta primary action color and one supporting warm secondary tone.

Avoid competing “primary” values that make button/state semantics ambiguous.

## 11. Preserve Soft Editorial breathing room

The Home should feel more editorial after removing the full service filter/menu.

Use the recovered space for:
- stronger section rhythm;
- larger imagery where valuable;
- cleaner copy;
- fewer competing cards.

## 12. Accessibility

Show clear, visible states for:
- keyboard focus;
- mobile menu control;
- primary/secondary CTAs;
- links over or near imagery.

Ensure gallery text remains readable without depending on low-contrast image overlays.

## Output

Provide:
1. refined mobile Home first;
2. refined desktop Home second;
3. updated design-system notes for any changed color/type/navigation tokens.

Do not add:
- new pages;
- new business rules;
- online payment;
- marketplace/branches;
- reviews/ratings;
- loyalty;
- packages;
- CRM;
- new staff roles;
- engineering/WordPress architecture.

The goal is to **tighten the accepted Soft Editorial direction**, correct product mismatches, and produce a Home/design-system candidate that can be considered for baseline freeze.
