Refine the **Gallery / نمونه‌کارها** page only.

The old Gallery already has a strong six-item editorial grid. Improve it without making it sparse and without turning it into a consultation/clinic experience.

## What to select in Stitch

Select exactly:

1. the current **old Gallery / نمونه‌کارها** screen with the six image cards;
2. the frozen **Home / خانه** screen as the primary editorial visual anchor;
3. the frozen **Services / خدمات** screen for public header/footer/CTA consistency.

Return **one Gallery screen only**.

## Source roles

Use the selected **old Gallery** screen for:

- six-item content density;
- 3-column × 2-row image-led grid;
- category/filter row;
- specialist attribution;
- concise per-item metadata.

Use frozen **Home + Services** for:

- visual system;
- public header/navigation;
- typography;
- spacing;
- content width;
- buttons;
- borders/radii/shadows;
- footer.

Do not copy Home or Services composition.

## Hard coverage requirement

Keep a complete representative portfolio.

Show **6 gallery items** in the default desktop view.

Prefer the existing balanced 3-column × 2-row composition.

Do not collapse Gallery into one featured image or a sparse editorial landing page.

## 1. Page intro

Use:

- title: `نمونه‌کارهای سالن آرا`
- concise neutral supporting copy.

Good direction:

**`گزیده‌ای از نمونه‌کارهای خدمات سالن آرا در دسته‌های مختلف.`**

Do not claim:

- international specialists;
- no-retouch guarantees;
- no-damage materials;
- medical/health benefits;
- legal/media consent status.

## 2. Category row

Keep a restrained filter/category row.

Use neutral categories such as:

- همه نمونه‌ها
- مو و استایل
- رنگ و لایت
- ناخن
- مژه و ابرو
- آرایش
- فضای سالن, optional

Do not use `اسپا` as a product positioning category.

Do not generate filter interaction states yet.

## 3. Gallery cards

Each card should include:

- large image;
- category label;
- short work title;
- concise neutral visual description;
- specialist name;
- optional short `جزئیات اجرا` metadata;
- CTA.

Use CTA:

- `رزرو نوبت`
- or `مشاهده خدمت مرتبط`

Do not use `رزرو مشاوره` as a new workflow.

## 4. Neutral descriptions

Describe visible style/appearance only.

Avoid claims such as:

- `بدون تخریب فولیکول`;
- `شفافیت پایدار`;
- `ارگانیک`;
- `اکسیژن‌رسانی سلولی`;
- `ماساژ لنفاوی`;
- `تقویت کلاژن`;
- therapeutic/clinical language;
- material-certification or proprietary-formula claims.

Do not turn the card into a treatment record.

## 5. Specialist attribution

Keep attribution simple:

- `متخصص: [نام]`

Use fictional neutral names.

Do not use:

- senior/master hierarchy;
- rankings;
- ratings;
- certificates;
- “international specialist” claims.

## 6. Remove the old consultation/analysis funnel

Do not preserve the old block about:

- facial/bone-structure analysis;
- skin-tone analysis;
- digital drafts;
- sensitivity testing;
- home-care routine;
- sample/material booklet;
- consultation appointment.

Replace that entire lower section with one restrained closing product CTA.

Example structure:

### `از نمونه‌کارها تا رزرو`

Short copy:

**`خدمت موردنظر را بررسی کنید، متخصص دلخواه را انتخاب کنید و رزرو نوبت را ادامه دهید.`**

Actions may be:

- `مشاهده خدمات`
- `رزرو نوبت`

## 7. Media/privacy wording

Do not show claims that all images:

- have verified consent;
- were captured in a special room;
- are unfiltered.

Keep the page as a visual prototype portfolio.

Production media rights will be verified separately.

- exact opening hours;
- north-Tehran prestige positioning;
- `خدمات تخصصی`;
- `رزرو آنلاین وقت`.

Use neutral placeholder/contact wording.

## Product boundaries

Do not add:

- reviews/ratings;
- social feed features;
- likes/comments;
- loyalty;
- CRM;
- consultation workflow;
- spa/clinic positioning;

- online payment/deposit;

The result should feel:

- editorial;
- complete;

Preserve useful density.

Do not over-decorate and do not make it sparse.
