# Stitch Prompt 07 — About page

Design the **About page** for the existing Persian/RTL brand **آرا**, using these frozen visual references:

- `ara-home-soft-editorial-v1`
- `ara-services-soft-editorial-v1`
- `ara-specialists-soft-editorial-v1`
- `ara-gallery-soft-editorial-v1`

Do not redesign the visual system. Extend the same Soft Editorial language.

## Product purpose

The About page explains the salon's identity, positioning and team context.

Its job is to help a potential customer understand:

- what آرا is;
- the kind of experience the salon aims to provide;
- how the team and service philosophy fit together;
- how to continue to Services, Specialists or Booking.

This is a trust/brand page, not a place to invent unverifiable proof claims.

## Visual continuity

Carry forward:

- Persian/RTL-first composition;
- warm ivory / taupe surfaces;
- restrained terracotta primary actions;
- editorial whitespace;
- natural photography;
- subtle borders/elevation;
- same header/navigation behavior;
- clear «وقت‌های من» access;
- prominent «رزرو وقت» CTA.

Use **سالن زیبایی بانوان آرا** positioning only.

Do not introduce:
- spa;
- clinic;
- medical center;
- atelier positioning.

## Page structure

### Intro / story

Create a strong editorial opening with:

- «درباره آرا»;
- concise salon story/positioning placeholder copy;
- emphasis on calm, clarity, personal choice and a considered beauty experience.

Do not invent:

- founding year;
- number of years in business;
- customer counts;
- awards;
- “best salon” claims;
- certificates;
- celebrity clientele;
- branch history.

If chronology is needed visually, keep it generic or omit it.

### Philosophy / values

Present a small set of grounded values such as:

- توجه به انتخاب و سلیقه مشتری;
- زمان‌بندی شفاف;
- اطلاعات روشن درباره خدمت، مدت و قیمت;
- امکان انتخاب متخصص;
- تجربه آرام و منظم.

Avoid guarantees such as:

- perfect results;
- zero waiting;
- fully sterile/certified environment;
- organic/vegan-only materials;
- medical safety guarantees.

### Team context

Introduce the team as the group behind the service experience.

You may show:

- selected specialist portraits;
- names;
- non-clinical expertise;
- link to «مشاهده متخصص‌ها».

Do not repeat the full Specialists catalog.

Do not add:

- ratings;
- awards;
- exact experience years;
- medical titles;
- unverified credentials.

### Salon experience / space

Use imagery to communicate the intended atmosphere of آرا.

Important:

- imagery is prototype/reference only;
- do not state that it is the real salon;
- do not invent a real address, branch history or physical feature list as approved facts.

Use neutral placeholder copy around calm, privacy, order and comfort without certification or guarantee claims.

### Product bridge

Make the About page connect naturally to the actual product.

Provide clear paths to:

- Services;
- Specialists;
- Booking.

You may include a concise explanation that customers can choose:
- one or more compatible services;
- a preferred specialist or «هر متخصص در دسترس»;
- an available date/time;
- then verify mobile identity and confirm.

Keep this summary light; do not turn About into another Booking page.

### Final CTA

End with a calm CTA such as:

- «خدمات آرا را ببینید»
- «متخصص خود را انتخاب کنید»
- «رزرو وقت»

Retain:
- payment happens at the salon;
- no online payment is required.

## Responsive behavior

Provide:

1. mobile About page around 390 px CSS width;
2. desktop About page around 1440 px CSS width.

The mobile page should preserve editorial rhythm and not become a long stack of identical cards.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for primary interactions.

Show:

- visible focus states;
- readable Persian typography;
- meaningful image alt-text intent;
- sufficient contrast;
- links/CTAs that remain clear without color alone;
- layouts that reflow cleanly.

## Media boundary

All images remain prototype/reference media.

Do not imply:
- the people are actual آرا staff;
- the space is the real salon;
- the pictured work or environment belongs to the business.

Production content/media will require explicit approval and rights clearance.

## Product boundaries

Do not add:

- reviews/ratings/testimonials;
- awards/certificates;
- founding/history facts;
- customer counts;
- multiple branches;
- spa/clinic positioning;
- medical/clinical claims;
- loyalty;
- ecommerce;
- packages;
- online payment/deposit;
- standalone customer Profile scope;
- admin/staff tools;
- architecture/API/storage decisions.

## Content truth

All names, portraits, story copy and business facts are placeholders unless separately approved.

Keep them visibly neutral and grounded.

## Output

Return:

- mobile About screen;
- desktop About screen;
- any design-system delta needed specifically for editorial story sections, values and team-preview treatment.

The page should feel like the natural brand-story extension of the frozen Home/Services/Specialists/Gallery system, not a new visual direction.
