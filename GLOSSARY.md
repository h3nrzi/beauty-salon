# NOIR Auto Detailing

Shared language for the detailing studio website and its appointment-request flow.

## Language

**Appointment Request**:
A customer's expression of interest in a detailing appointment, subject to later confirmation by the studio. It is not a confirmed appointment or a reservation of an available time slot.
_Avoid_: Booking, allocation, commission

**Service**:
A detailing offering: Exterior Detail, Interior Detail, Full Detail, Paint Correction, or Ceramic Coating.
_Avoid_: Protocol, service tier, Custom Consultation

**Project**:
A completed example of detailing work on a vehicle, represented by photography and a caption or description. A project demonstrates the studio's work; it is not a separate service offering.
_Avoid_: Commission, allocation, provenance record

**Preferred Date**:
The customer's requested date for an appointment, subject to the studio's confirmation. It does not indicate availability or reserve a slot.
_Avoid_: Reserved date, confirmed date

**Studio**:
The NOIR Auto Detailing business and its physical detailing location.
_Avoid_: Atelier, concierge

**Testimonial**:
A customer's account of their experience with the studio.
_Avoid_: Provenance registry
